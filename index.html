Acorn Rain

For nine nights, a strange rain of acorns falls, and every acorn it touches wakes up. Alive. Now they're coming for every living thing. One lands on a squirrel. It changes him too, but he still fights for the living. He's the shield between the world and the swarm.

There's even a board game hidden in the village.
